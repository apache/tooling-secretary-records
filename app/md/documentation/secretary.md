The Secretary and the Assistant Secretary can do everything the tool offers,

## ICLAs from the submission form

Somebody with no account can fill in and sign an ICLA at `/icla`, or follow an
invitation there. They sign it one of three ways: by typing their full name and
agreeing to the terms, by signing the filled-in PDF and uploading it, or by
uploading the PDF with a detached PGP signature of it.

Each one is mailed to `secretary@apache.org` with the ICLA attached, as an
emailed ICLA arrives: replying goes to the submitter. A typed signature says,
in the mail, the name typed, when, and from which address. Neither the PDF
signature nor the PGP signature is checked yet. Each is also kept, for the work
queue to pick up when it arrives; until then the mail is what you work from.
